## Conclusion

The stable object, once every later retraction is applied, is small and definite. It is cochain algebra on a product of cycles, an integral Künneth realisation of \((p,p)\) classes on that torus, a cubical \(L^2\) bound of \(2^d\) on uniform grids, a heap charging argument whose two-level corollary does not follow from its own display, an unrefereed semi-discrete Ricci–DeTurck estimate on one mesh of the flat 4-torus, and a pigeonhole on a finite index set. Each folder’s later file narrows an earlier slogan onto one of those objects and leaves the slogan’s original target tagged open: the synthesis’s analytic stage becomes one cubical construction; “unconditional” becomes an equivalence with the Hodge conjecture for \(A_J\); “verified two ways” becomes an eigenvalue that one Lemma G copy derives and does not machine-check; “fully rigorous” continuum limit becomes a chat sketch that a later numerical cosine contradicts; “we prove” the 4-torus estimates becomes a transplant whose source draft still excludes the pullback to Ricci flow. The practical rule these folders support is that a usable citation names the file, the tag, and the hypothesis class. The file dated the next morning often keeps the hypothesis and withdraws the victory sentence.
