## Which classes, on which manifolds, in which codimension

### Takeaway
The proved realization (Theorem A) is every integral Künneth class of type (p,p), in every codimension p, on the MRH digit torus, by wall intersections or by products of U(1) first Chern classes or by c_p of a diagonal U(p) connection. For a general complex structure J, codimension 1 is claimed proved (discrete Lefschetz). Higher codimension on the associated abelian variety is Conjecture B and is tagged Open. The notes do not claim Hodge classes on a general projective manifold.

### Inferences
- What is actually claimed as proved, without an external theorem and without a hypothesis class, is the rational and integral Künneth (p,p) ring on the digit torus, plus the codimension-one case for any complex structure J via Landau line bundles (Appell–Humbert / F3).
- Conjecture B does not enlarge the manifold class beyond abelian varieties built from a complex structure on H^1 of that torus. A proof of B^∞ would be a proof of the Hodge conjecture for those abelian varieties, and conversely. It would not be a proof for a general smooth projective variety.

## Bi-flatness, Künneth splitting, Whitney forms, and the MRH

### Takeaway
Künneth splitting is the bigrading for which the conjecture is claimed proved. Bi-flatness is the curvature condition that cuts out that bigrading for a lattice connection. Whitney forms are the comparison map W with RW = id in the setup, and later a tool in the gauge estimates. The MRH is the place-value order on the digit torus that fixes orientation signs; the heap papers are a separate priority-queue filtration. None of the four, by itself, is offered as a proof of the classical Hodge conjecture.

### Cited Findings
- **Künneth.** §0: “The Künneth bigrading β_K is H^d(T;ℚ) = ⊕_{a+b=d} H^{a,b}_K, where H^{a,b}_K = span{e_I : |I∩ℛ| = a, |I∩𝒞| = b}. Discrete (p,p) = H^{p,p}_K. Under the discrete Hodge isomorphism this is ker L_{2p}.” T1(5): the basis of H^{p,p}_K is intersections of p row walls and p column walls. — [foundation §0 and §2](file:///C:/Users/grego/Downloads/Research/02%20Discrete%20Layered%20Hodge%20Conjecture/Discrete_Layered_Hodge_Conjecture_foundation.md)

### Inferences
- Bi-flatness is what makes a non-abelian lattice field stay inside the Künneth (p,p) summand. Without a type constraint, F5(2) says holonomy represents every even rational class and the Hodge statement is empty. The paper says this in §5.4.
